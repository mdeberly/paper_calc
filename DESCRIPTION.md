# Paper CALC — the calculator made for your Mudita Kompakt

**A calm, crisp calculator designed for E Ink, not adapted to it.**

Most calculator apps are made for bright, colorful screens. On an E Ink display they turn into
ghosting, faded gray buttons and animations that never quite finish. Paper CALC is designed from
the ground up for the Mudita Kompakt: pure black on white, large, sharp digits, and nothing
that moves when it doesn't have to.

## Made for E Ink

- **High-contrast black & white**: no colors, no gradients, no ripple effects. Every key is
  sharp and readable, even in direct sunlight.
- **No animations**: the screen only refreshes when something actually changes, which means
  less ghosting and less battery use.
- **A bigger display**: the keypad is deliberately compact so the operation and its result get
  more room.
- **Haptic feedback on every key**: a short vibration confirms each press, so you never wonder
  whether the E Ink screen registered your tap.

## Smart, but simple

- **Live result**: the answer updates as you type, with no need to press "=".
- **Correct order of operations**: `2 + 3 × 4 = 14`, the way it should be. Results are exact
  (no `0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000004` surprises).
- **One smart parentheses key**: it knows whether to open or close a parenthesis for you.
- **f(x) mini keypad**: π, e, square root, x², powers, 1/x, sign change and factorial, one tap
  away and hidden the rest of the time.
- **History**: press "=" to save an operation. Your last 20 calculations are kept. Tap one to
  reuse it, long-press to copy it.
- **One-tap copy**: send the result, or the full "operation = result", to the clipboard.

## Make it yours

- **Decimal separator**: comma or point (`3,14` or `3.14`).
- **Thousands separator**: point, comma or space (`1.234.567`, `1,234,567` or `1 234 567`).
- **Copy button**: copies the result only, or the operation followed by its result.
- **Haptic feedback**: on or off.
- **10 languages**: English, French, Polish, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Czech
  and Swedish, switchable inside the app.

## Light and respectful

- No ads, no account, no tracking, and **no internet permission**.
- No Google services required: works on the de-Googled Kompakt out of the box.
- A tiny APK with no third-party libraries.

## Install

1. Download `PaperCALC.apk` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/mdeberly/paper_calc/releases/latest).
2. Open it on your Kompakt (allow installation from unknown sources if asked), or run
   `adb install PaperCALC.apk`.
3. To check for updates later, go to **⋮ → About → Check for the latest release**.
